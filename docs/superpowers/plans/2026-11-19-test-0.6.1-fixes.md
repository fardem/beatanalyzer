## Review Focus

- Drag ends with the mouse left of / right of the overview → seek clamps to 0 / duration (test in Task 1).
- Click on the frame without moving, then double-click → no seek from mouseup, play from left edge (Task 1: `isDragMove` false below 4 px).
- Loop "section" active while dragging → no seek on release (Task 1 wiring: skip when `followPlayhead` is false).
- Stored default window that is longer than the song (e.g. 60 s, song 40 s) → not applied, whole song shown (Task 2 test).
- Corrupt localStorage value (e.g. `"abc"`, `"7"`) → treated as no default (Task 2 test).
