# BeatAnalyzer 0.6.1

The main changes after 0.5.1 – versions 0.6.0 and 0.6.1. All details are in the [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Highlights

### Song structure that hears the music
- Every bar is compared with every other by its **notes (chords)**, its **sound colour (instruments)** and its
  loudness. Section changes are found even when the chorus is as loud as the verse.
- **Parts that come back get the same letter** (A, B, A, B, C …) – in the web app, in the Resolve markers
  ("Section B ▲ +5 dB – Bar 33") and in the Audacity labels.

### Better tools for cutting
- **Insert and delete beats** – in the beat table, or Shift+click / Alt+click in the zoomed waveform; undo also in the
  beat table header.
- **Sample-exact metronome**, and the note next to the song volume **mutes the song** to hear only the metronome.
- **One main tempo everywhere** (tile, tempo curve, histogram) – parts counted at half or double speed no longer move
  it. The beat interval tile shows how much the grid wobbles ("Grid ± 4 ms").
- **Double-click on the blue frame** of the overview plays the section; band lanes switch between 1× and 2× height.
- **MIDI export with a tempo map** – every click on its beat in a DAW.
- Help texts with **dots in the colours of the diagrams**.

### DaVinci Resolve
- **Own colours for cue marks and section changes** in *Adjust marker colours* (default yellow and purple).
- The marker JSON, the note of the first marker and the subtitle info block name **BeatAnalyzer and its version**.

### Security and clean-up
- The local server **only answers its own web app** – other websites open in the browser can no longer trigger an
  update, install the Resolve script or change settings. Uploads go to safe temp files; the song name stays as it is.
- Cleaner signal processing (zero-phase filters, no aliasing); the old stand-alone page and leftover development
  settings are gone.

### Fixed
- The song volume sometimes did nothing after a few plays.
- The "straight eighths / swing" line was removed – it was never measured.

## Updating

1. Get the new version (update in the web app, `update.bat` or the ZIP) and run `start.bat`.
2. In the web app: *Export → DaVinci Resolve* → **Update in Resolve**, then restart Resolve – needed for the new colour
   fields and the version in the markers.

## Known

- The Resolve window needs DaVinci Resolve **Studio**.
- The song structure is computed from the sound, not a musical analysis – listen before you rely on it.
- Very long files (DJ sets) take a moment after the analysis while the song structure is computed.
